# Q3 methodology and interpretation

## Scope
State × industry analysis covers employing SMBs at June 2025. Grant status is recorded at 22 October 2026, with VIC-1, NT-1 and NT-2 rechecked on 30 and 31 October. Dates describe that snapshot, not a live eligibility service.

## Panels
1. **Reach grid:** the colour shows the modelled non-adopter pool; labels show programme reach under the selected definition. Whole-industry reach excludes restricted partial-industry coverage. Unconfirmed coverage can be shown or hidden. Federal support is presented separately.
2. **Entry/exit scatter:** x = entry rate, FY2025; y = exit rate, FY2025. Both axes run from 0% to 15%. Above the equality line means exits exceed entries; below means entries exceed exits. Industry is encoded by colour. NSW Finance has entry 11.7%, exit approximately 6.4%; ACT Manufacturing has entry approximately 6.5%, exit 13.0%.
3. **Open programmes:** nine programmes in the recorded open-window snapshot. NT Cyber Invest (NT-1) has a $20,000 business cap, opened 5 October 2026, closes 15 January 2027 at 3:30 pm ACST. NT Grow Your Business remains $50,000, closing 13 November 2026. Caps describe individual business support; they are never summed or weighted. Programme pool budgets are excluded.
4. **Cluster mix:** shares are weighted by SMB counts within the clustered population of each state. LGAs outside the model are excluded from this denominator. Cluster labels are provisional; they are not proof of causal adoption effects.

## Controls and aggregation
The saved-view selector provides preset views. Grid selections update state and industry parameters. Programme details provide the underlying reach evidence. There are 28 referenced programme IDs, with three industry mappings each; 84 programme-industry rows do not mean 84 programmes. The counting definition yields 22 programmes.

Support mix is parsed from a text field containing cash, service and project-scale flags; those indicators are classifications, not budgets. Entry and exit percentages must not be summed across state-industry rows. Federal totals, repeated programme caps and repeated cluster denominators must not be added across rows.

## Reference totals and limits
The 24 state-industry rows sum to 223,037.38 SMBs. Whole-industry Manufacturing reach is NSW 1 and VIC 0. NSW plus VIC PST SMBs total 94,508 of 150,073. Minor differences from whole-number brief totals reflect rounding. Modelled opportunity does not demonstrate grant effectiveness, conversion or causal impact. Source programme restrictions and current eligibility must be checked before business decisions.
